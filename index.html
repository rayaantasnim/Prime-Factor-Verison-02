The Cognitive Philosophy

How Prime-Factor V2.0 retrains the human mind for sub-second algorithmic number theory recognition.

Mental Sieve Conditioning

By forcing rapid trial-division elimination of small primes (2, 3, 5, 7, 11), competitors develop intuitive residue class recognition, drastically collapsing search space before consciously computing.

Modular Reflex Loops

Iterative speed sessions condition the motor-cognitive path. Competitors learn to execute modulo operations as visual pattern matches rather than multi-step arithmetic divisions.

Stress-Induced Accuracy

Under accelerated timer pressure and penalty multipliers, the brain shifts from speculative guessing to rigorous verification—forging extreme precision under competitive cognitive load.
